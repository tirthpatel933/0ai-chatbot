Private by design

0AI runs the Llama 3.2 1B open model in your browser using WebGPU. Messages, conversation history, and attached files are processed on this device and saved only in this browser’s local storage. They are never sent to an AI service.

To start, the app downloads its open-source engine from jsDelivr and model files from Hugging Face; browser caching can make future visits faster. File readers (PDF.js, Mammoth, SheetJS, and Tesseract.js for image text) are also loaded from jsDelivr when needed. Your chat and files are not sent to these providers. The model may be slower or unavailable on devices without a compatible browser, WebGPU, or enough memory. Clearing this site’s browser data removes saved conversations and cached files.
